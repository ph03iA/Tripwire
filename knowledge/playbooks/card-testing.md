# Playbook: card testing

## How it looks
- Stolen card details are checked with tiny charges, ₹1 to ₹10, at several different merchants within minutes. Some are declined.
- When the card works, a large purchase follows, often electronics, within the hour.

## Signals that matter
- Three or more charges under ₹50 at three or more merchants in 30 minutes.
- A charge of ₹10,000 or more within an hour of such a burst.

## Response
- Block the large purchase that follows a testing burst.
- Step up, or watch, the burst itself.

## Look-alikes
- A customer adding a card to several apps at once triggers ₹1–₹2 verification charges, sometimes three in a few minutes. No large purchase follows.
- Single small charges such as top-ups and subscriptions are normal.
