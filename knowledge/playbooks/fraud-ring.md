# Playbook: fraud ring on one device

## How it looks
- One device signs in to several different accounts over a day or two, hours apart.
- Each account then sends a moderate amount (₹8,000–₹15,000) to the same new payee, which collects the money.
- On its own, each account looks like a customer with a new phone paying someone new.

## Signals that matter
- A device used by other accounts in the last 7 days. The more accounts, the stronger the signal.
- A transfer to a new payee that other accounts have also just paid for the first time.

## Response
- Once a device is seen on two or more accounts, block transfers from it to new payees.
- Share the device and payee with every agent, so the payment decision knows what the device check saw.

## Look-alikes
A family sharing one tablet. They pay known payees, not the same brand-new one from every account.
